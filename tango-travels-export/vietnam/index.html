MORNING — ARRIVAL

Marble Mountains

Landed in Đà Nẵng and went straight to the Marble Mountains. Rode the elevator up, then explored the caves and pagodas on foot — a five-tiered pagoda rising out of the trees, a cavern lined with hundreds of small Buddhist figures, a soaring natural skylight overhead, and a stone Buddha seated in a mossy hollow.

Walked back down. Tiring, but one of the most beautiful starts to a trip.

Pancakes, then Bánh Mì Madam Khánh

Lunch was at a local spot for bánh xèo (pancake), spring rolls and skewers, then bánh mì at Madam Khánh — "the Queen" — which lived up to its name.

A nap, then the river

A well-earned afternoon nap, then out to the river for a lantern boat ride under Hội An's glowing bridges — one of the most photogenic hours of the day.

A beer, then the best scallops

A pint at the Irish pub with a live band playing — a toast to the birthday. Dinner after at an Argentinian steakhouse — El Gaucho — for what might be the best scallops yet, alongside a good bottle of wine.
